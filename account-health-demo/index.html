Account Health Monitor

A browser extension I built for the daily marketplace monitoring routine. For every brand it switches account and country, reads Account Health, Performance Notifications and support cases, skips anything already reported, then drafts the emails and the team tracker. It never sends anything on its own. Press Start all brands to watch.

What it replaced

The clicking is automated, the judgement stays human

Before Every day, by hand

  1. Switch to the next brand, then to Canada
  2. Open Account Health and read the status and any issues
  3. Open Performance Notifications and find today's messages
  4. Repeat both for the United States
  5. Search US support cases for new Amazon replies
  6. Check the team chat so nothing is reported twice
  7. Write each email and update the tracker by hand
  8. About 30 brand and country switches per round

After With Account Health Monitor

  1. Press Start all brands
  2. Review the findings, untick anything that shouldn't count
  3. Open the drafted emails, check them, send
  4. Copy the tracker into the team chat, then press Save to Data

It only reads. It never sends an email or message, never changes anything in the account, and pauses for a person if the site asks for sign-in or verification.
