Lean Six Sigma DFSS Green Belt

Design for Six Sigma · 20-hour program · October 25, 2026

Most Six Sigma training teaches you to fix a process that already exists. Design for Six Sigma (DFSS) is about building something new so that it works from day one. The Green Belt covers DMADV, a 15-step roadmap that starts with what the customer actually needs and ends with a tested, documented process.

It's how I approach the tools I build: start from what the team needs, turn it into requirements I can measure, compare options before building, then verify the result and write the SOP so others can run it.
